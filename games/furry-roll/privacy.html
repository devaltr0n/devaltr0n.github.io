$game privacy policy

Effective $updated. Applies to $game for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by $studio.

$game is built to collect as little as possible. There are no ads, no third-party analytics, no tracking across apps, and we never sell or share your data. You never give us an email address, phone number or real name.

Playing without the online features

Your fursonas, coins, settings and purchases are stored only on your device. The only things that can leave it are the anonymous usage stats and any feedback you send, both described below.

Usage stats

To learn which parts of the game people use and where they get stuck, the game counts what happens in it: which screens are opened, rolls, catches, battles, pictures saved, shop visits and purchases (which item and whether it went through, never payment details), how long the game took to start, and the app version and phone type. Each event is a name and a few small numbers or words, sent to our own server (Supabase, below) with your anonymous account ID.

There are no names, no messages, no contacts, no location and no advertising IDs. No analytics company is involved, and nothing is used for ads or to track you across apps. You can turn usage stats off any time in Settings › Share usage stats; anything not yet sent is then deleted from your phone. Stats are kept for up to a year, or until you delete your online data.

Feedback

Settings › Send feedback sends what you type, the kind you picked (bug, idea, love it or other), the app version, your phone model and system version, and a few game numbers (how many fursonas you have and the graphics setting). Please don't include your name, address or other personal details. We use feedback only to fix and improve the game. It is stored with your anonymous account ID and deleted with your online data.

The online features

Friends, cloud backup, the daily world board, meet-ups, hangout rooms, the area board and the adoption board use our server. When you use them, the game signs you in with an anonymous account: a random ID, with no email, phone number or name. We store:

  • your random account ID and a friend code;
  • the nickname you choose, if any. Only friends who have added your code, and whose code you have added, see it;
  • your fursonas' codes, names and rarity: your cloud backup, which those friends can also see;
  • your daily challenge scores. The public board shows a name made up from the fursona's code, never your nickname;
  • meet-ups: a short code that lasts 90 seconds, and which two accounts have met;
  • hangout rooms you open or join: the room's 4-letter code, the fursona codes in it and its votes. Other members see made-up fursona names only, never nicknames. Rooms close after a day;
  • area board posts: the area you picked from a list (for example a state or a country), a fursona code and one ready-made shout. There is no free text, and reported posts are hidden;
  • fursonas you give up for adoption: the fursona's code and rarity, so another player can adopt it. No names or account details are shown.

Nobody can send you free-typed messages in $game. The game never reads your device's location: the area board only uses the area you choose.

This data is stored by our hosting provider, Supabase, and always sent over encrypted connections.

Photos

When you tap a save button in the Photo Studio, the game asks to add pictures to your photo library. It can only add the pictures you choose to save. It cannot see, read or upload your photos.

Notifications

Notifications are off until you say yes. If you turn them on, the game can remind you when an egg is ready to hatch and, on a day you haven't played, when your free Cosy Boxes are back. They are never sent between 8 pm and 8 am. These reminders are local: your phone shows them on its own. There is no notification server and no push ID, and nothing about them leaves your phone. You can turn them off any time in the game's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through the App Store or Google Play. We never see your payment details. The store only tells the game what was bought.

Deleting your data

  • Online data: Settings › Delete my online data deletes your anonymous account and everything stored with it, including usage stats and feedback, straight away.
  • On your device: uninstalling the game deletes everything stored on the device.
  • You can also email $email and we will help.

Children

$game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The online features never ask for personal information such as a name, email address, contacts or location. A parent or guardian who has a question, or who wants a child's online data deleted, can email $email.
