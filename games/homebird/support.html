Common questions

How do I restore the unlock on a new device?

Open the unlock page in the Grown-ups corner and choose to restore your purchase. The unlock is tied to the Apple or Google account that bought it, so the new device must use the same account.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store. On iPhone or iPad, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play's refund help.

How do I reset progress?

In the Grown-ups corner, use "Reset progress and settings". Deleting the app also deletes everything. See the privacy policy.
