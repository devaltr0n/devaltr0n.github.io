Common questions

I bought coins but didn't get them

Close the game completely and open it again while you're online: purchases the store has finished are added when the game starts. If they still don't appear, email us the receipt from Apple or Google (it shows the order ID) and we'll sort it out.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone or iPad, go to reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, open your order history in Google Play, or see Google Play's refund help.

I got a new phone. How do I keep my fursonas?

On your old phone, make sure the online features are on so your furries are backed up. On the new phone, open Settings › Restore furries from cloud. Coins bought from the store stay with your Apple or Google account. Use Restore purchases in the Shop's Store tab if anything you bought once is missing.

Photos won't save

The game needs permission to add pictures. On iPhone, go to Settings › Furry Roll › Photos and choose Add Photos Only or Full Access. On Android, allow storage or photos access for Furry Roll in the system settings.

Do purchases change the dice?

No. Everything you can buy is just for looks. The dice are the same for everyone.

How do I delete my data?

Open Settings › Delete my online data. It deletes your anonymous account and everything stored online, straight away. Uninstalling the game deletes everything on your device. See the privacy policy for details.

Someone posted something mean

Nobody can type messages in Furry Roll: boards only show fursonas and ready-made shouts. If a post still bothers you, report it in the game (three reports hide it) or email us.
