Homebird: Language Cards privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Applies to the Homebird: Language Cards app for iPhone, iPad and Android, made by Tiny Hearth.

Homebird collects nothing about your child by default. There is no account, no sign-in, no name or age entry, no photos of people, no advertising, and no analytics. The only exception is an optional pronunciation check that stays off until a parent turns it on; it is described below.

What stays on the device

  • Settings (the languages chosen, Kiki's look, sound on or off).
  • Progress: which words were heard and said, feathers earned, postcards made.
  • These are stored only in the app's own folder on the device and are deleted when the app is deleted. A grown-up can also reset them from the Grown-ups corner inside the app.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while your child practises saying a word, and only after you allow it in the system permission dialog. The recording (up to four seconds) is kept in memory so your child can hear it back, is replaced by the next attempt, and is never written to the device's storage.

Optional pronunciation check

  • Off by default. A grown-up can turn it on in the Grown-ups corner, which is behind a parental gate, and turn it off again at any time. Everything else in the app works without it and without an internet connection.
  • When it is on, the short recording of the word being practised is sent over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our server, forwarded to ElevenLabs for speech-to-text, compared with the word, and then discarded by our server. We keep no copy.
  • Nothing else is sent with it: no name, no account, no device identifier, no location. Only the audio and a language code.
  • ElevenLabs processes the clip under its own privacy policy and may retain it for the period that policy states.
  • A child's voice is personal information. We treat the parent's switch as consent for this single purpose and never derive voiceprints or keep recordings.

Purchases

The app offers one one-time purchase that unlocks all word worlds. Prices are shown only in the Grown-ups corner behind the parental gate, never on children's screens. Payment is handled entirely by Apple or Google; we never see payment details.

No third parties on children's screens

There are no advertising or analytics SDKs in the app and no links out of the app on children's screens. Crash reports, if any, come only from Apple and Google's own store tools.

Your choices

  • Delete all data: delete the app, or use "Reset progress and settings" in the Grown-ups corner.
  • Stop the pronunciation check: switch it off in the Grown-ups corner.
  • Revoke the microphone permission in your device settings at any time.

Changes and contact
